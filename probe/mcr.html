    Market Conditions Report

     

     

    The following information has been gathered from multiple sources and qualified by Future Electronics Product Marketing. The information is intended to provide guidance only and should be treated accordingly.

    Future Electronics offers world-class supply chain programs to mitigate market supply and demand volatility. Contact your Account Manager to discuss our solutions in more detail.

    View PDF

    Last updated: October 19th, 2026

    A word from Anthony Alberga, Corporate Vice President

    Upward Pressure on Lead Times Continues

    "Lead times continue to extend across most technology segments. Product categories currently facing - but not limited to - allocation from multiple vendors include DRAM, storage solutions, NOR Flash, small-signal and bipolar devices, MOSFETs, MLCCs, tantalum polymer components, aluminum Capacitors, Specialty Resistors and some selective Microcontroller and Analog Products.

    As market conditions continue to tighten, we anticipate further lead-time expansion and expect additional technology categories to experience similar supply constraints. Accelerated inventory depletion across both distribution channels and end customers, combined with limited demand visibility, continues to increase the risk of supply shortages across a broad range of critical components beyond those already mentioned.

    To help ensure continuity of supply, it is essential that we receive accurate and timely demand forecasts from our customers. We remain committed to supporting our customers through innovative, industry-leading supply chain solutions designed to improve resilience and mitigate future disruptions.

    We welcome the opportunity to discuss these strategies and explore how we can help secure your supply requirements."
